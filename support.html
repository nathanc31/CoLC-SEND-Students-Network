External SEND Support

These organisations support SEND young people across Liverpool, Merseyside, Wirral, the North West, and the wider UK.

SEND & Disability Support

  • Daisy Inclusive UK — disability support, inclusive sports, advocacy.
  • Deaf Active — youth clubs, community support and BSL activities for young people who may want to learn sign language.
  • MSDP (Merseyside Society for Deaf People) — Deaf services, communication support.
  • Liverpool SENDIASS — impartial SEND advice, EHCP support, advocacy.
  • NHS Learning Disability Services — specialist LD support.
  • Annual Learning Disability Health Checks — free yearly GP health checks for people on the LD Register.
  • NHS Speech & Language Therapy (SALT) — communication support.
  • NHS Occupational Therapy (OT) — sensory & daily living support.
  • NHS Physiotherapy (PT) — mobility & physical support.
  • Liverpool CAMHS / Neurodevelopmental Pathway — autism & ADHD assessments.
  • ADDvanced Solutions — neurodiversity support for families & young people.
  • National Autistic Society — autism support & advice.
  • Personal Independence Payment (PIP) — disability benefit.
  • Employment Support Allowance (ESA) — support for health-related work barriers.
  • Citizens Advice — benefits, housing, and legal guidance.
  • Liverpool SEND Local Offer — EHCP support & services.
  • Liverpool Adult Social Care — independent living support.
  • Short Breaks (Liverpool) — respite & activities.
  • IPSEA — EHCP legal advice.
  • Contact — SEND family support.
  • Scope — disability support & advocacy.
